1Roll six dice and place them in a row, in the order they came up.
2Subtract 1 from each. (Rolled a 1? It's now 0.)
3Multiply each result by its weight, left to right: 7776, 1296, 216, 36, 6, 1
4Add the six products together. That's your value.
5Divide by 22 and keep only the integer part (ignore any remainder). Result ≤ 2047? → that's your word! Result ≥ 2048? → re-roll.
💡 The weights are powers of 6: 6⁵=7776, 6⁴=1296, 6³=216, 6²=36, 6¹=6, 6⁰=1. You only need to memorize them once.
🔢 The index starts at 0. The division result is the word's position in the BIP-39 wordlist counting from 0: "abandon" = 0 … "zoo" = 2047. In the example, "satoshi" is word #1531.

If your printed wordlist numbers words starting from 1 ("abandon" = 1), look at row 1532: simply add 1 to the index.

Quick rule: check where "abandon" sits in your wordlist. If it's at position 1, add 1 to your result. If it's at position 0, use it as-is.
♻️ You'll almost never need to re-roll. A roll is rejected only when the value maps to an index ≥ 2048, which happens in about 3 out of every 100 rolls. If it happens much more often, check that your dice are fair.
